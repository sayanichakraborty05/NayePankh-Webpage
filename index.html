About Us

Who we are and what we stand for.

NayePankh Foundation was started in 2019 by a group of students who believed that every child deserves a fair chance in life. We work at the grassroots level across 14 states in India.

Our mission is simple — talent is everywhere, but opportunity is not. We bridge that gap through education, skill development, and health programs that are built with and for the communities we serve.

We are a DARPAN registered NGO with 80G and 12A tax exemption. 92% of all donations go directly to our programs.

Our Programs

Three core programs designed to support children at every stage of their life.

📚

Shiksha — Education

Free study centres, tutoring, and learning materials for children in rural and urban areas who lack access to quality education.

🛠️

Kaushal — Skills

Vocational training and digital literacy workshops for youth aged 14–21, giving them tools to earn a living independently.

🌱

Swasthya — Health

Nutrition drives, mental health awareness, and hygiene camps to build a strong foundation for children's physical wellbeing.
