Our Programs

How We Create Change

Three interconnected pillars, designed to support children through their formative years and into a self-sufficient adulthood.

📚

Shiksha — Education Access

From rural study centres to urban tutoring hubs, we remove the barriers that keep 260 million Indian children out of quality learning environments.

Education
🛠️

Kaushal — Skill Development

Vocational training, digital literacy workshops, and mentorship programs that equip youth aged 14–21 with tools for economic independence.

Skills
🌱

Swasthya — Health & Wellbeing

Nutrition drives, mental health first-aid training, and hygiene awareness camps that build a foundation of physical and emotional resilience.

Health

Voices of Change

Stories from the Field

Real words from the children, parents, and educators whose lives have shifted since NayePankh arrived in their communities.

Before NayePankh's study centre opened, I walked 8 km to school and still had nowhere to study at night. Now I'm preparing for my Class 12 boards with full confidence.

👧
Priya, 16
Shiksha Program · Jharkhand

The Kaushal program taught me mobile repair and accounting software. I started my own shop at 19. My mother cried the day I handed her my first month's earnings.

👦
Arjun, 21
Kaushal Program · Uttar Pradesh

As a volunteer teacher here, I've watched silent, withdrawn children become leaders of their classroom. NayePankh doesn't just teach subjects — it teaches children who they are.

👩
Ms. Fatima Ansari
Volunteer Educator · Bihar
